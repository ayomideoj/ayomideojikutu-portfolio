00 · About

A rare blend of design & code.

I design wireframes, high-fidelity interfaces and clickable prototypes — then build them into fast, responsive products end-to-end. Nothing gets lost in translation between design and code, because I'm the one holding both ends of it.

My process runs the same way on every project: research the user and the problem, map the flow, design the interface, then build and ship it. That loop — research, wireframes, UI, prototyping, engineering — is what lets me move a product from a blank page to something people can actually use.

I work across the stack with React, Next.js and Node.js, and across the design tools with Figma, treating both as one continuous craft rather than two separate jobs handed off between people.
