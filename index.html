A rare blend of design & code.

I design wireframes, high-fidelity interfaces and clickable prototypes — then build them into fast, responsive products end-to-end. Nothing gets lost in translation between design and code, because I'm the one holding both ends of it.

My process runs the same way on every project: research the user and the problem, map the flow, design the interface, then build and ship it. That loop — research, wireframes, UI, prototyping, engineering — is what lets me move a product from a blank page to something people can actually use.

I work across the stack with React, Next.js and Node.js, and across the design tools with Figma, treating both as one continuous craft rather than two separate jobs handed off between people.

Case Study

Reel Board

A kanban-style production tracker for filmmakers — cards move through Pitch, Development, Pre-Production, Production and Post & Release as a project advances. Built as a self-initiated React project.

Visit live ↗
reel-board.vercel.app
The Problem

Film projects move through distinct, non-linear stages, and generic task managers don't speak that language — there's no native sense of "format," no quick way to see what's stuck, and no lightweight way to pitch, tag and track a slate of productions at a glance.

The Approach

A five-column board (Pitch → Development → Pre-Production → Production → Post & Release) with drag-and-drop cards, live search across titles and tags, filtering by format, and a per-card "days in stage" indicator so nothing quietly stalls.
